This policy explains what personal data {{company.tradeName}} collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it in plain language. If something is unclear, email us at {{company.privacyEmail}}.

## Who we are

{{company.tradeName}} is run by {{company.legalName}}, registered in {{company.country}} under number {{company.registrationNumber}}, with its registered address at {{company.address}}.

{{company.tradeName}} sells NFC and QR "review cards" and a feedback dashboard to businesses such as restaurants, cafés, salons, gyms, hotels, shops and clinics. A customer taps a card or scans a QR code, gives the business a rating from 1 to 5 stars and can then choose to leave a Google review.

## The short version

- **If you rate a business:** we store your star rating and when and how you gave it. We do not store your name, email, phone number, IP address, device fingerprint or location. The rating pages set no cookies.
- **Every customer gets the same choice.** Whatever rating you give, you are offered the same optional "Leave a Google Review" button. We never publish your rating to Google, and we never write or post reviews.
- **If you have a business account:** we store what you need to sign in and run your account, plus the business information you enter.
- **We do not sell personal data**, and we do not use advertising pixels, third-party analytics or device fingerprinting.

## Our role: who is responsible for what

Data protection law usually separates two roles:

- A **controller** decides why and how personal data is used.
- A **processor** handles personal data for a controller, following the controller's instructions.

Here is how that works for {{company.tradeName}}:

- **Ratings collected for a business.** Each business decides to collect feedback from its customers using our cards. For these ratings, the business is the controller and we are its processor: we store and show the ratings for that business and do not use them for our own purposes.
- **Business data a business enters** (its name, logo, address, opening hours and so on) belongs to the business, and the business is responsible for it.
- **Our own data.** We are the controller for business account data (such as your sign-in email), billing records, contact and demo requests, marketing emails, data deletion requests and security data.

Ratings do not contain names or contact details, so in most cases nobody, including us and the business, can tell who left a particular rating.

## What we collect

### When you rate a business (customers)

When you tap a card, scan a QR code or open a business's feedback link, we store only:

| Data | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Your rating | 4 stars |
| Which business you rated | An internal business ID |
| Which location or card was used, if any | An internal location ID or card ID |
| How you arrived | NFC card, QR code or link |
| When you rated | Date and time |

We also count, per business and per day, how many times its page was viewed, how many NFC taps and QR scans happened, how many ratings were submitted and how many people clicked the Google review button. These counters are stored **without** IP addresses or browser details.

We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, IP address, device fingerprint or location in this flow, and these pages set no cookies.

### Business account users

If you create or are added to a business account, we store:

- your name and email address;
- your password, stored only in hashed (scrambled) form by our authentication provider, so we cannot read it;
- the businesses you belong to and your role (owner, manager or viewer);
- whether you are a {{company.tradeName}} staff administrator.

For the business, we store the information you enter, for example: business name, logo, description, brand colours, address, opening hours, phone number, email, website, social media links, Google review link, locations and cards. Some of this information (such as name, logo, address, phone, website, opening hours and social links) is shown on the business's public feedback page, because that is its purpose. Only enter information you are happy to show to customers in those fields.

We also store subscription records (plan, billing period, status) and invoices (number, amount, dates, status).

### Contact and demo requests

If you use our contact or demo request form, we store your name, email, business name (optional), your message and whether you ticked the box to receive marketing emails. The box is not ticked by default.

### Marketing emails

We send marketing emails only to people who have opted in. For this we store your email address, where you signed up and a code that lets you unsubscribe in one click.

### Data deletion requests

If you ask us to delete data, we store your email, what you want deleted, any details you give us, and the status of your request.

### Security data

To stop abuse (for example, someone sending thousands of fake ratings), we limit how often requests can be made. For this, we store a salted SHA-256 hash of the IP address (a one-way code that does not show the IP address itself) for **at most 1 day**.

Our hosting provider processes standard request data, such as IP address and browser type, for a short time in order to deliver the website. We record important actions in the dashboard and admin area (for example, who changed a setting and when) in an audit log.

## Why we use your data and our legal basis

The law requires us to have a valid reason ("legal basis") for each use of personal data. In plain words:

| What we do | Data used | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store and show ratings and counters for a business | Ratings, counters | We act for the business, which decides the basis (usually its legitimate interest in understanding customer satisfaction) |
| Create and run your business account, sign you in, provide the dashboard | Account and business data | **Contract**: we need it to provide the service you signed up for |
| Issue invoices and manage subscriptions | Account, subscription and invoice records | **Contract** and **legal obligation** (accounting and tax rules) |
| Answer contact and demo requests | Contact request data | **Legitimate interests** (answering people who contact us) and steps you asked for before a contract |
| Send marketing emails | Email, sign-up source | **Consent**, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Prevent abuse and keep the service secure | Hashed IP addresses, audit logs, request data | **Legitimate interests** (keeping the service and its users safe) |
| Handle data deletion and privacy requests | Request data | **Legal obligation** |
| Remember your language choice for longer than one visit | Language cookie | **Consent** (see our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies)) |

## What we never do

- We never publish ratings to Google or anywhere else.
- We never write, post or edit reviews for anyone.
- We never show the Google review button only to happy customers. Everyone gets the same option.
- We never sell personal data or use it for advertising.
- We never use third-party analytics, advertising pixels, social media embed scripts or device fingerprinting.

## Who can see your data

- **Members of a business account** see only their own business's ratings and statistics (for example the number of ratings, the average and the daily trend). Because ratings contain no personal details, they cannot see who left a rating. They cannot see other businesses' data.
- **{{company.tradeName}} staff with an admin role** can access business and account data when needed to run the service, give support, handle billing, deal with abuse or meet legal duties.
- **Service providers (subprocessors)** that host and run the service for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage) and Vercel (hosting). See our [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page for details.
- **Google** receives nothing from us. If you choose to tap "Leave a Google Review", your browser goes to the business's Google review page and Google's own privacy policy applies from then on.
- **Banks.** Invoices are currently paid by bank transfer, so the banks involved process the payment details needed for the transfer.
- **Authorities**, if the law requires us to share data.

## International transfers

This means your data may be processed outside {{company.country}}. When this happens, we rely on the safeguards offered by our providers and on the transfer rules that apply to us.

## How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Customer ratings | 24 months by default. Each business can choose between 1 and 60 months. |
| Daily counters (views, taps, scans, ratings, Google button clicks) | 24 months by default. Each business can choose between 1 and 60 months. |
| Hashed IP addresses for rate limiting | At most 1 day |
| Business account and business profile data | While the account is open. After an account is deleted, personal data is removed within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep. |
| Subscription records and invoices | As long as accounting and tax law requires |
| Audit logs | 24 months |
| Contact and demo requests | 12 months |
| Marketing email data | Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it |
| Data deletion requests | As long as needed to show we handled the request |

When a retention period ends, we delete the data.

## How we protect data

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal data. For example:

- we collect as little data as possible, so there is less to protect;
- all connections to the website are encrypted (HTTPS);
- passwords are stored only in hashed form;
- access to business data is limited by role, and the database itself checks that each user can only reach their own business's data;
- we limit the rate of requests to prevent abuse;
- important dashboard and admin actions are recorded in an audit log.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise that data will never be accessed without permission. If you think there is a security problem, please email {{company.privacyEmail}}.

## Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you can:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **export** it. Business users can export their ratings as CSV or JSON from the dashboard. For other data, ask us for a copy;
- **correct** data that is wrong. You can update most account and business details yourself in the dashboard;
- **delete** your data;
- **object** to how we use your data, for example when we rely on legitimate interests;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, for example by unsubscribing from marketing emails or changing your cookie choices. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew it;
- **complain** to {{company.supervisoryAuthority}}.

### How to use your rights

- Email {{company.privacyEmail}}, or
- use the [data deletion form](/data-deletion), or
- if you have an account, go to **Dashboard → Privacy**.

We may need to check your identity before we act on a request, to protect your data from other people. We aim to reply within 30 days.

**If you are a customer who left a rating:** ratings are not linked to your name or contact details, so we usually cannot find which rating is yours. If you still have a question, contact us or the business directly. For ratings, the business is the controller, so we may pass your request on to it.
