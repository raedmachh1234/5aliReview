# Refund Policy

_Last updated: {{lastUpdated}}_

This policy explains when and how {{company.tradeName}} gives refunds. It applies together with our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It does not reduce any rights you have under the law.

## How billing works today

We currently issue invoices manually and you pay by bank transfer. Refunds are also paid by bank transfer, normally to the account the payment came from. Prices exclude VAT (TVA), which is added at the applicable rate and shown before you pay.

## Free trials

Trials are free and do not require payment details. When a trial ends you choose whether to subscribe — nothing is charged automatically. Because you pay nothing during a trial, there is nothing to refund.

## Subscriptions and cancellation

- Cancel anytime from **Billing** in your dashboard in one click.
- Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. There is no cancellation fee.
- After you cancel, you will not be invoiced again.
- We do not normally refund the unused part of a monthly or yearly period, because you keep full access until it ends.

We **do** refund the unused part of a period you paid for if:

- we close your account or stop the service when you have not broken our terms; or
- we remove a major paid feature and you cancel because of it.

## Price changes

We will email you before any price change takes effect. If you do not want to continue at the new price, cancel before the change applies and you will not pay the new price.

## Billing errors and duplicate payments

If we charged you the wrong amount, invoiced you after you cancelled, or you paid the same invoice twice, we will **always refund** the amount paid in error once we have verified it.

## NFC cards

### Defective cards

If a card does not work when it arrives, or stops working under normal use, tell us. Once we have confirmed the problem, we will replace it free of charge.

### Returning unused cards

For additional cards you bought separately, we offer a **14-day return window**: if a card is unused and undamaged, you can return it within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the card price. If your cards were custom-printed for you (for example with your logo), we can only accept a return if they are defective. Unless the card is defective, return shipping is at your cost.

### Lost or stolen cards

If a card is lost or stolen, you can deactivate it from your dashboard so it stops working. Lost or stolen cards are not refunded, but you can order replacements.

## How to ask for a refund

Email {{company.billingEmail}} with:

- your **invoice number**;
- the business name on your account;
- a short explanation of the problem;
- for cards: the card label and, if possible, a photo.

## Timelines

- We aim to reply to every refund request within **5 business days**.
- Once a refund is approved, we aim to send it within **10 business days**. Banks may take a few more days to show it in your account.

These are our targets. If something takes longer, we will keep you informed.
