# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: {{lastUpdated}}_

This policy explains which cookies {{company.tradeName}} uses and how you can control them. The short answer: very few, and none for analytics or advertising.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. They let the site remember things, for example that you are signed in or which language you chose.

## Cookie categories

| Category | In use? | Can you turn it off? | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | Yes | No | Needed for the site to work, such as keeping you signed in and remembering your cookie choices |
| Preferences | Yes | Yes | Remembering your language between visits |
| Analytics | **No** | — | We do not use analytics cookies |
| Marketing | **No** | — | We do not use marketing or advertising cookies |

Essential cookies do not need your consent, because the site cannot work properly without them. We only use preference cookies for longer than your current visit if you allow them.

## The cookies we use

This is the complete list. We do not set any other cookies.

| Name | Category | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sb-<project>-auth-token` | Essential | Keeps business owners and admins signed in to the dashboard. Only set after you sign in. | Session; refreshed while you stay signed in |
| `5ali_demo_session` | Essential | Signs you in to the demo dashboard (demo mode only). Only set after you sign in. | 8 hours |
| `5ali_consent` | Essential | Remembers your cookie choices so we don't ask on every page. | 6 months |
| `5ali_lang` | Preferences | Remembers the language you picked. Without preference consent it is kept only until you close the browser. | 1 year with consent; otherwise until the browser is closed |

In the first cookie name, `<project>` stands for our internal project identifier at our database and sign-in provider, Supabase.

## Customer feedback pages set no cookies

The pages customers see after tapping a card or scanning a QR code — where they choose a star rating — set **no cookies at all**.

## Third-party cookies

None. We do not load content from other companies, such as analytics tools, advertising pixels or social media embeds, so no other company can set cookies through our site.

If you choose to tap "Leave a Google Review", you leave our site and go to Google. Google may then set its own cookies under its own policies. That happens only after you leave our site, and we have no control over it.

## How to change your choices

- Click **"Cookie settings"** in the footer of any page to change your choices at any time.
- If you withdraw consent for preference cookies, your language choice will be kept only until you close the browser.
- You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, you may not be able to sign in to the dashboard.

When we change the cookie categories we use, we will ask for your choices again.
